# Engineering Standards

These standards are binding for all Engineering OS workflows. Agents must cite them when planning and reviewing work.

## Hierarchy

1. [Discovery standards](discovery-standards.md)
2. [Reuse standards](reuse-standards.md)
3. [Planning standards](planning-standards.md)
4. [Verification standards](verification-standards.md)
5. [Change control standards](change-control-standards.md)

## Universal rules

1. Follow the active workflow phase; do not skip ahead to implementation.
2. Prefer repository conventions over generic AI defaults.
3. Do not invent backend APIs, schemas, environment secrets, or infrastructure.
4. Record approved deviations in the Decision Log.
5. Keep changes scoped to the Feature Contract (or bug contract).
6. Produce evidence artifacts; do not claim completion without them.
